# Philip Meisner Zwisler

Hey, I'm a Software Developer and Senior DevOps Engineer at Twoday.

My journey into tech started with Windows XP and a lot of curiosity. I spent countless hours poking around the family computer, often breaking it and having to fix it again. That taught me my mantra: "The computer only does what you tell it to."

Today I use that curiosity to build and stabilize systems. As a consultant at IT Minds I bridged the gap between legacy systems and modern technology: migrating Jenkins setups to Azure DevOps, automating infrastructure, renovating observability and developing .NET solutions.

I began with an AP Degree in Computer Science at KEA, and became CTO of Teoriundervisning.dk with full technical responsibility. When I'm not in code, I go fishing, cook or game.
